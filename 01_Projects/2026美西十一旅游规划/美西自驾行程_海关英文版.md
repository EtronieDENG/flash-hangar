## Day-by-Day Schedule

### Day 1 — Sep 26 (Sat): Arrive LAX → Costco → Friend's Home
- **14:50** Flight lands at LAX.
- **15:40–16:20** Free shuttle to Hertz rental base; pick up SUV.
- **16:20–17:15** Drive north on I-405 to San Fernando Valley (~30 km; allow 50–60 min for Saturday afternoon traffic).
- **17:15–18:30** Costco shopping (Sherman Oaks / Van Nuys store) — water, soda, dry food.
- **18:45** Arrive at friend's private residence, unload luggage and supplies. Dinner nearby.

### Day 2 — Sep 27 (Sun): Pasadena → Griffith Observatory
- **08:30** Depart friend's home via CA-134 E.
- **09:00–09:45** Pasadena: **The Gamble House** (exterior photos); **Colorado Street Bridge**.
- **10:00–13:00** **The Huntington Library** (opens 10:00). Desert Garden; lunch at 1919 Cafe ~11:30.
- **13:15–16:30** **Caltech** campus store; Old Pasadena — bookstore, afternoon tea.
- **16:45–18:00** Dinner at **The Cheesecake Factory** (Old Pasadena).
- **18:40** Park at **Barnsdall Art Park**; take DASH Observatory shuttle bus up to **Griffith Observatory**.
- **19:00–20:30** Griffith Observatory night view of LA; shuttle back, return to friend's home.

### Day 3 — Sep 28 (Mon): UCLA → Venice Beach → Beverly Hills → Hollywood
- **08:30** **UCLA** campus visit.
- **11:30–13:30** **Venice Canals** & **Venice Beach** graffiti walls (park at LADOT Lot 731).
- **14:00–15:30** **Beverly Hills** — Rodeo Drive (park 450 N Rexford Dr garage, first 2 hrs free); photo at Beverly Hills sign.
- **15:45–17:45** **Petersen Automotive Museum**; **Hollywood Walk of Fame**.
- **17:45+** Return via US-101 (reverse commute, smoother); pack at friend's home.

### Day 4 — Sep 29 (Tue): The Getty → San Diego
- **08:30** Depart friend's home.
- **09:30–11:30** **The Getty** (advance reservation for Tuesday entry & parking).
- **11:30–13:40** Drive south on I-5 to La Jolla, San Diego (~190 km, ~2h10m).
- **13:40** Check in La Jolla hotel; unload luggage.
- **14:00–18:00** **UC San Diego** (wooden footbridge); **La Jolla Cove** sea lions; **Hodad's** burger ~15:00; **Old Town San Diego**; **USS Midway Museum** & **Unconditional Surrender** statue at harbor.
- **18:15** **Tacos El Gordo** (takeout).
- **18:45–19:30** **Imperial Beach Pier** sunset; drive back to La Jolla hotel (~30 min).

### Day 5 — Sep 30 (Wed): Cross-State to Las Vegas via Mojave Desert
- **09:00** Check out; drive north on I-15.
- **10:15–11:00** **San Bernardino Costco** — fill fuel, snacks (last cheap fuel before leaving CA).
- **11:00–11:15** Original **McDonald's Museum** (exterior photo), San Bernardino.
- **11:15–15:30** Through **Mojave Desert**; lunch at **Barstow** ~12:40; **Baker** "World's Tallest Thermometer" ~14:30 (photo from car); **Mojave National Preserve** visitor center; Joshua Tree forest.
- **15:30–16:45** **Seven Magic Mountains** (photo); **Las Vegas Welcome Sign**.
- **17:15** Arrive **Trump Hotel Las Vegas**, valet parking.
- **Evening** The Sphere; steak dinner; Bellagio Fountains.

### Day 6 — Oct 1 (Thu): Bighorn Sheep → Hoover Dam → Route 66 → Grand Canyon
- **08:00** Check out; depart.
- **08:45–09:15** **Hemenway Park** — wild bighorn sheep.
- **09:30–10:30** **Hoover Dam** (security check); dam bridge (time-zone dividing line).
- **10:30–14:10** Hwy 93 → I-40; **Kingman** ~12:10; **Grandpa's Kitchen** burger ~13:00.
- **14:10–16:30** Historic **Route 66**; **Peach Springs** restroom ~15:10; **Seligman** colorful town ~15:30; fill fuel ~16:30.
- **16:45–17:30** Drive north on Hwy 64 to Grand Canyon (daylight, safe wildlife timing).
- **17:30** Arrive **Tusayan** hotel; check in.

### Day 7 — Oct 2 (Fri): Grand Canyon Sunrise → Horseshoe Bend Sunset
- **07:00** Check out (luggage in trunk); enter South Rim ~07:15 (no queue, morning light).
- **08:30–11:50** **Rim Trail** walk (Mather Point etc.).
- **11:50–12:20** Eat in car; depart 12:20.
- **12:20–14:20** East exit via Hwy 64; **Grandview Point**; **Desert View Watchtower** ~13:35; exit east gate ~14:05.
- **14:20–16:30** US-89 north to **Page** (red-rock "sunset highway").
- **16:30** Arrive Page (go straight to Horseshoe Bend, skip hotel first).
- **16:40** **Horseshoe Bend** parking.
- **16:50–18:15** Walk 1.2 km trail to cliff; sunset ~18:11.
- **18:30+** Check in Page hotel; Texas BBQ dinner.

### Day 8 — Oct 3 (Sat): Antelope Canyon → Red Canyon → Bryce Canyon Stargazing
- **08:45** Breakfast, check out.
- **09:00–11:00** **Upper / Lower Antelope Canyon** (guided tour, morning light).
- **11:00–12:15** Lunch in Page.
- **12:30–15:30** US-89 north into Utah; **Red Canyon** twin tunnels (UT-12) ~14:30; arrive **Bryce Canyon City** hotel ~15:30.
- **15:30–20:30** Check in, rest; sunset viewpoint ~16:30; dinner ~18:30.
- **21:00+** Stargazing at **Sunrise Point** — Milky Way.

### Day 9 — Oct 4 (Sun): Zion → Las Vegas Gun Range → Ontario (CA)
- **07:00** Depart Bryce Canyon.
- **08:30** Through **Zion-Mount Carmel Tunnel** (no queue).
- **12:15** Arrive Las Vegas (gain 1 hr time zone).
- **12:30–14:30** **Gun range** (live-fire shooting, 2 hrs).
- **14:30–17:30** Drive southwest to **Ontario, CA** (~3h, avoid Sunday evening return traffic).
- **17:30** Check in Ontario hotel; repack luggage.

### Day 10 — Oct 5 (Mon): Outlet Shopping → LAX Departure
- **09:30** Breakfast, check out.
- **10:00–12:30** **Ontario Mills** outlet (Coach, Michael Kors, Nike, etc.), ~2.5h.
- **12:30** Drive to LAX (~1h15m).
- **13:45** Arrive LAX rental return — ~3h05m before 16:50 flight.
- **14:00** Shuttle to terminal; check bags; security.
